Architecture

How this differs from ordinary record lookup

Global Patient Record Project patterns

  • Simple create, append, and history workflow
  • Opaque identifiers rather than names
  • Immutable event and fragment objects
  • Parent links forming an auditable history
  • Offline-oriented local operation

Health-specific protections

  • Two factors are required for every operation
  • Authenticated encryption instead of AES-CBC
  • Clinician signatures and patient authorization MACs
  • FHIR resources rather than generic descriptions
  • No public history URL or demographic patient search

Google health and biometric SDKs

Google Open Health Stack and the Android FHIR SDK can support FHIR forms, offline storage, workflows, and synchronization. They do not provide patient fingerprint identity or fingerprint-derived key material.

Android BiometricPrompt can protect a clinician or device key using biometrics enrolled on that Android device. It does not expose raw fingerprints and cannot identify arbitrary patients presenting at a shared clinic tablet. A field deployment therefore needs a separate patient-biometric SDK and supported scanner.
